FLORIDA TINT LIMITS, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

These are the state limits we tint to on every Pasco County vehicle. The number is how much light still passes through the glass and film together.

CARS & SEDANS

Front side windows: at least 28%. Rear side and back window: at least 15%.

SUVS, TRUCKS & VANS

Front side windows: at least 28%. Rear side and back window: down to 6%.

WINDSHIELD

Non-reflective tint above the AS-1 line (the visor strip). Clear ceramic heat film is the comfort option below it.

PASCO COUNTY QUESTIONS

Do you cover both west and east Pasco? +

Yes. We regularly travel to New Port Richey, Hudson and Trinity on the west side and Land O’ Lakes, Wesley Chapel and Dade City toward I-75, with no extra travel fees.

Will window film break my HOA rules? +

Most HOA rules are about reflective or mirrored looks. Neutral, low-reflection films usually keep the outside of the house looking the same. We recommend checking your HOA guidelines before booking.

Can you tint my company’s office windows in Wesley Chapel? +

Yes. Commercial film is installed on site like everything else we do. Request a commercial estimate and tell us about the glass and your schedule.

Is 5% legal on the back of my SUV? +

Yes. On SUVs, trucks and vans, Florida allows the rear side and back windows down to 6%. The front side windows must stay at 28% or lighter.
