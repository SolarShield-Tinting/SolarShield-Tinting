4. Window Film Curing & Care Instructions

Following installation, automotive tint requires an initial drying and curing period:

  • Do NOT roll down windows for a minimum of 3 to 5 days (allowing moisture to fully evaporate).
  • Minor haziness, water pockets, or small moisture bubbles are a natural part of the curing process and will dissipate in the Florida sun within 1 to 3 weeks.
  • Clean tinted windows only with soft microfiber towels and ammonia-free cleaners. Never use abrasive scrubbers or ammonia-based sprays (like traditional Windex).

Florida Tint Statutes • Informational Notice

Florida Window Tint Law Compliance

Window tint laws vary by state and vehicle classification. Under Florida Statutes §§ 316.2951 – 316.2956:

  • Sedans / Passenger Cars: Front side windows must allow at least 28% Visible Light Transmission (VLT). Rear side and rear window must allow at least 15% VLT.
  • SUVs, Trucks & Vans: Front side windows must allow at least 28% VLT. Rear side and back window may be tinted to 6% VLT (privacy glass).
  • Windshield: Non-reflective tint allowed above the manufacturer's AS-1 line (visor strip). Clear ceramic heat rejection film across the full windshield is subject to state medical exemption rules and legal guidelines.

*Customer acknowledges that selecting tint darkness levels below statutory limits is done at customer's own discretion and responsibility. SolarShield Mobile Tinting strongly recommends and installs Florida-legal shades.
